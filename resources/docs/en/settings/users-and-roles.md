Every person who signs in to your shop has a user account and one role. The role decides which menus they can see and what they can do. Only people with the settings permission (the Owner by default) can manage users.

## Add a user

1. In the sidebar, under **Settings**, open **Users**.
2. Press **Add**.
3. Type the **Name** and **Email**. The email is what the person types to sign in, and it must not be used by anyone else.
4. Pick a **Role**.
5. Type a **Password** of at least 8 characters, and tell the person privately.
6. Press **Save**.

The top of the page shows how many users you have, for example "3 of 10 users". If you reach the limit, adding another is refused until you upgrade. See [Plans compared](help:plans/plans-compared).

## What each role can do

| Role | Can see and do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including **Settings**, **Users** and **Integrations**. |
| **Manager** | Everything except **Settings**, **Users** and **Integrations**. |
| **Cashier** | **Dashboard**, **POS**, view products and sales, view and add customers. A Cashier can also close the till. |
| **Storekeeper** | Products, stock, warehouses, purchases and suppliers. Cannot use the **POS**. |
| **Accountant** | Sales, customers, reports, expenses, and can view stock, purchases and suppliers. An Accountant can view every till closing. |

Menus that need the Medium plan, such as **Reports** or **Purchases**, only show if your plan includes them.

## Change or remove a user

1. Open **Users**.
2. To edit, press the pencil icon. You can change the name, email and role. Leave **Password** empty to keep the current one, or type a new one to reset it.
3. To remove, press the bin icon and confirm.

> **Note:** You cannot delete your own account, and the shop must always keep at least one Owner. You also cannot change the last Owner to another role.

> **Tip:** If someone forgets their password, edit their account here and type a new one. See [I forgot my password](help:troubleshooting/forgot-password).

If a user cannot see a menu they expect, read [I cannot see a menu](help:troubleshooting/cannot-see-a-menu).
