The audit log is the shop's memory. Every time someone adds, changes or deletes something important, the system writes a line saying who did it, when, and what changed. Nobody can edit or remove these lines, not even the Owner.

## Open the log

1. In the sidebar, under **Settings**, open **Audit log**. It needs the Enterprise plan and the **Audit log** permission, which only the Owner has by default.
2. The newest entries are at the top. Each line shows **When**, **Person**, **Action**, the **Record** and its **Details**.
3. Press **Details** on a line to see each field that changed, as the old value, an arrow, and the new one. Amounts are shown in your shop currency.

## What is recorded

- Products (for example a changed selling or cost price), customers, suppliers and warehouses.
- Sales, sale returns, purchases and stock transfers.
- Expenses and till closings.
- Manual stock adjustments, with the reason. Stock that moves because of a sale or purchase is not repeated here; it belongs to that sale or purchase.
- Staff accounts and their roles, and custom roles.

Passwords are never written to the log.

## Find something

Use the filters at the top: a date range, the kind of **Record**, the **Action** (created, changed, deleted, role changed), the **Person**, or type part of a name or number in **Search**. Press **Filter**; press **Reset** to start again.

## Good to know

- The log starts recording on every plan, so when a shop upgrades to Enterprise its earlier history is already there.
- If you move to a smaller plan the log keeps recording but you cannot open it until you are on Enterprise again.
- An entry with the person shown as **System** was made without anyone signed in, for example by an import or a background task.
- If a staff account is removed later, their name stays on the entries they made.
