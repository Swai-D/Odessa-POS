---
title: Signing in and out
summary: How staff sign in to their shop, and what happens when a session ends.
keywords: login, sign in, logout, session, expired, shop address, password
order: 20
---
Staff can always sign in at the main address with their own email and password. You do not need to open the shop's own link first: the system finds your shop from your account.

- **Signed out or session expired:** open the main sign-in page and enter your email and password as usual.
- **Signing out** takes you back to your shop's sign-in page, which shows the shop's name.
- **Seeing another shop's name** on the sign-in page (shared computer)? Press **Not your shop?** and sign in with your own account.
- Shops with their own web address (a custom domain or subdomain) only accept that shop's staff on that address.
