We send the shop owner an email and an SMS when the subscription is about to end, and a receipt when a payment is recorded.

## Get the SMS
1. Open **My Profile** from your name at the top right.
2. Type your mobile number, for example `0712 345 678`.
3. Click **Save**.

Reminders go out 7 days and 3 days before the end date, on the day, and on the last day of the grace period. Your data is never deleted, even when a subscription ends.

## Low-stock messages
Every morning at 8:30 we check your products. If some are at or below their **alert quantity**, you get one email and SMS listing them. You are not told again about the same list until it changes or a week passes. Set the alert quantity on each product to choose which ones are watched.
