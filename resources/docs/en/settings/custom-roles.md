Besides the five default roles, the Enterprise plan lets you build your own. A role is simply a list of what its owner may see and do, so you can give a supervisor more than a cashier but less than a manager.

## Create a role

1. In the sidebar, under **Settings**, open **Roles**.
2. Press **Add**.
3. Type a **Role name**, for example "Supervisor". It must be different from the default names and from your other roles.
4. To save time, pick a role in **Start from a role**. Its permissions are ticked for you and you can change them.
5. Tick what this role may do. Each area has **View** and often **Add, change and delete**.
6. Press **Save**.

The role now appears in the **Role** list when you add or edit a user. See [Add staff and choose their roles](help:settings/users-and-roles).

## Change or delete a role

- Press the pencil icon to change the name or the ticked permissions. People who already have the role get the new permissions at once.
- Press the bin icon to delete a role. A role that still has users cannot be deleted: give those users another role first.

## Good to know

- The default roles (Owner, Manager, Cashier, Storekeeper, Accountant) are shown for reference and cannot be renamed, changed or deleted.
- Ticking **Settings and users** lets that role manage staff and roles, so give it only to people you trust as much as an Owner.
- A permission only matters if your plan has the feature. A role with **Reports** still cannot open reports on a plan without them.
- If your shop later moves to a smaller plan, your custom roles and their users are kept and keep working. You just cannot edit them until the plan includes custom roles again.
