The **Settings** page holds the details that appear on your receipts and the optional features your shop uses. Only people with the settings permission (the Owner by default) can open it.

## Change the business details

1. In the sidebar, open **Settings**.
2. In the **Business** box, type your **Business name**. This is the name shown on receipts.
3. Pick your **Currency**. The choices are TZS, KES, UGX, USD and EUR. New shops start with TZS.
4. Optionally, type a **Receipt footer**, for example "Thank you, come again". It can be up to 300 characters and is printed at the bottom of every receipt.
5. Press **Save**.

> **Note:** The currency is used for sales made from now on. Think before you change it in a shop that already has sales and products priced in another currency.

## Optional features

Under **Optional features** you will see switches. They are all off until you turn them on:

- **Weighed products** lets you mark a product as **Sold by weight**, so it can be sold in fractions such as 1.5 kg.
- **Batch tracking** adds a **Track batches** switch to the product form.
- **Expiry tracking** adds a **Track expiry dates** switch to the product form.
- **Barcode scanner** and **Cash drawer** are listed here too. A barcode scanner already works in the till search box without any setting (see [Printers and barcode scanners](help:troubleshooting/printing-and-scanners)). Nothing is connected to a cash drawer yet.

After switching a feature on and saving, open a product (see [Add a product](help:products/add-a-product)) and you will find the new switch in its **Optional features** box.

## Printers, fiscal receipts and mobile money

These are not on this page. Printers are set up under **Integrations**. Read [Integrations](help:settings/integrations).

> **Tip:** If you cannot see **Settings** in the sidebar, your role does not include it. Ask the Owner, or read [Users and roles](help:settings/users-and-roles).
