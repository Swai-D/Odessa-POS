Customer records let you sell on credit and see who owes you. Walk-in buyers do not need one.

## Add a customer

1. In the side menu, under **Peoples**, click **Customers**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Type the **Name** (the only required field). You can also fill in **Phone**, **Email** and **Address**.
4. Type a **Credit limit** if you want to cap how much the customer can owe. Leave it empty for no limit.
5. Keep **Active** switched on, then click **Save**.

You can also add a customer straight from the till: click the add-person icon, type the **Name** and **Phone** and click **Save**. See [Make a sale](help:selling/make-a-sale).

## Find, edit and tidy up

- Use the search box to find a customer by name, phone or email.
- Click the pencil icon to change a record, then **Save**.
- The list shows **Outstanding** (what the customer owes) and **Credit limit**.

## Deleting a customer

You can delete a customer with the trash icon only if they have no sales. If they do, the system says **This customer has sales and cannot be deleted. Mark them inactive instead.** Open the record, switch **Active** off and save. Inactive customers stay in your history.

## Who can do this

- Viewing customers needs the customers view permission; adding and editing needs the manage permission. Owner, Manager and Cashier can add and edit customers by default.
- The Accountant can look at customers but not change them.

> **Tip:** Write the phone number the same way every time (for example 0712 345 678) so the search finds it.
