A customer's balance is the unpaid part of all their sales. It goes up when you sell on credit and down when they pay or return goods.

## See who owes you

- **Customers** list: the **Outstanding** column shows each customer's balance.
- **Sales** list: filter by **With balance due** to see each unpaid sale.
- **Reports**: the card **Customers who owe** lists the biggest balances, and you can download it as CSV. It needs the Medium plan or higher. See [Reports](help:money/reports).

## Set or change a credit limit

1. Open **Customers** and click the pencil icon next to the customer.
2. Type the amount in **Credit limit**. Empty means no limit; 0 means no credit at all.
3. Click **Save**.

At the till, a credit sale is refused if the new balance plus what they already owe is over the limit. See [Credit sales](help:selling/credit-sales).

## Collect a payment

Payments are recorded against a sale, not against the customer:

1. Open the sale from **Sales**.
2. Under **Record payment** choose the **Method**, type the **Amount** and click **Save**.
3. Repeat for other unpaid sales of the same customer.

The sale status changes from **Unpaid** to **Partly paid** to **Paid**.

> **Note:** There is no single "pay all" box yet. If a customer gives you a lump sum, record it against their oldest sales one by one.

> **Tip:** Check the **Customers who owe** list each week and call the biggest balances first.
