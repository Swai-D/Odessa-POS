There is no "reset by email" yet on the sign-in page, so a password is reset by a person who can manage users.

## If you are a staff member

1. Ask your shop Owner to reset it.
2. The Owner opens **Settings > Users**, clicks the pencil icon next to your name, types a new **Password** (at least 8 characters) and clicks **Save**.
3. Sign in with the new password and change it to your own: see below.

## If you are the Owner

Contact whoever supplies the system. The platform administrator can reset the Owner account.

## Change your own password

1. Open the profile menu at the top right and click **My Profile**.
2. In **Change password**, type your **Current password**, then the **New password** and **Confirm new password**.
3. Click **Change password**. You will see **Your password has been changed.**

## Cannot sign in even though the password is right

- Check the email address is exactly the one the Owner entered.
- Make sure you are on your own shop's address (for example your shop name followed by the system domain). A user can only sign in to their own shop.
- Check Caps Lock.

> **Tip:** Use a password only you know, and never share one account between two people. The sales list shows who served each sale, and shared accounts hide that.
