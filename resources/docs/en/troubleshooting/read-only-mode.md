If you see **Subscription expired** or **Your subscription has expired, so changes are switched off**, the shop is in read-only mode.

## What is happening

The paid-until date and the 7-day grace period have both passed. To protect the business, the system stops anything that changes data, but you can still look and print. Your data is safe and nothing is deleted.

## What still works

- Sign in, open sales, receipts, reports, stock, products and customers.
- Print receipts and download PDFs and CSV files.

## What is refused

New sales at the till, payments, returns, adding or editing products, stock changes, purchases, expenses, users and settings.

## How to fix it

1. Check the banner or the bell at the top for the date.
2. Pay whoever supplies the system and ask them to record the payment. See [Renewal, grace period and read-only mode](help:plans/renewal-and-grace).
3. Reload the page. You can sell again straight away.

## "Shop switched off"

This is different. The platform administrator switched the shop off, so the shop does not open. The message says **This shop has been switched off. Please contact us.** Contact whoever supplies the system.

> **Tip:** If you are in the middle of a shift and the till refuses to sell, write the sales on paper and enter them after you renew. Keep the paper.
