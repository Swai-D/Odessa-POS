## Selling

**Can I change the price at the till?** No. Prices come from the product. Change them in **Products**. See [Make a sale](help:selling/make-a-sale).

**A customer paid part now and part later. How?** Choose the customer and pay the smaller amount; the rest is credit. See [Credit sales](help:selling/credit-sales).

**I made a mistake on a sale.** Sales cannot be edited. Record a return, then sell again. See [Returns and refunds](help:selling/returns-and-refunds).

**Can I put a customer's order aside?** Yes, use **Hold**. See [Hold and resume an order](help:selling/hold-and-resume).

## Stock and products

**Why can't I sell more of a product?** The warehouse has no more of it. See [Not enough stock](help:troubleshooting/not-enough-stock).

**How do I add many products at once?** Import a CSV file. See [Import and export](help:products/import-export).

**Stock count is wrong.** Correct it with [Adjust stock](help:stock/adjust-stock).

## Staff and access

**A staff member cannot see a menu.** See [I cannot see a menu](help:troubleshooting/cannot-see-a-menu).

**Someone forgot their password.** See [I forgot my password](help:troubleshooting/forgot-password).

**How many staff can I add?** It depends on your plan. See [What each plan includes](help:plans/plans-compared).

## Money

**Where is my profit?** Under **Reports**, in **Profit and loss** (Medium plan). See [Read your reports](help:money/reports).

**My till count does not match.** See [Close the till](help:money/close-the-till).

## Subscription

**It says my subscription expired.** See [Renewal, grace period and read-only mode](help:plans/renewal-and-grace).

## Hardware

**My printer or scanner does not work.** See [Printers and barcode scanners](help:troubleshooting/printing-and-scanners).

## Not available yet

Mobile money collection (the customer paying from their phone through the system) and TRA fiscal receipts are not built yet. You can record that a customer paid by mobile money, but the system does not collect or confirm the money.
