What you see depends on three things: your **role**, your **plan**, and whether you are signed in to the right shop.

## Check in this order

1. **Is there a lock icon on the menu?** The item is in the menu, dimmed. Your plan does not include it. Click it to see which plan has it. See [What each plan includes](help:plans/plans-compared).
2. **Is the menu simply missing?** Your role does not allow it. For example a Cashier does not see **Reports**, **Purchases** or **Settings**. See the role table in [Users and roles](help:settings/users-and-roles).
3. **Did you get "403" or "You do not have permission"?** You opened a page your role cannot use. Ask the Owner.
4. **Is a button missing inside a page?** Buttons like **Add**, **Record payment** and **Return items** need the permission to manage that area. Viewing is separate from changing.
5. **Is the page there but saving is refused?** Your shop may be read-only. See [Why is my shop read-only?](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).

## Who can fix it

The Owner opens **Settings > Users**, edits the person, and changes their **Role**. The change works at their next page load. If the Owner cannot see **Users** either, the platform administrator who supplies the system can help.

> **Note:** The default roles are fixed: Owner, Manager, Cashier, Storekeeper and Accountant. If a person needs a mix, give them the closest role.

> **Tip:** Ask the person to sign out and sign in again if a change does not show.
