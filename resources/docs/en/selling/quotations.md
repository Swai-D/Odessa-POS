A quotation is a price offer. It does not take stock or money until you turn it into a sale.

## Make one
1. Open **Sales → Quotations** and click **New quotation**.
2. Pick the customer, or type a name if they are not a customer yet.
3. Add products and quantities. Prices, tax and the discount are worked out when you save.
4. Click **Save quotation**, then **Download PDF** to send it.

## When the customer says yes
1. Mark it **Accepted** (or **Sent** / **Declined** to keep track).
2. Click **Convert to sale**, choose where the stock comes from, and enter what was paid. Leave it empty to sell on credit for one of your customers.

The sale uses **today's prices**. If prices changed since the quotation, you will see a warning with both totals. A quotation past its **valid until** date cannot be converted; make a new one.
