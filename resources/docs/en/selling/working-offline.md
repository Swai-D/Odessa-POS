The till keeps working when the internet drops. Sales are saved on the computer or phone and sent by themselves when the connection is back.

## Get ready (once)
1. Open the till (**Sales → POS**) while online. The product and customer list is saved on the device, for every branch you can sell from. It refreshes by itself every few minutes.
2. Optional: install it. In Chrome or Edge choose **Install Odessa POS** (the icon in the address bar, or the browser menu; on a phone, **Add to Home screen**). It then opens like an app.

## When the connection drops
- The top bar shows **No connection**. Search, add to the basket and take payment as usual with **cash, card, bank or hand-typed mobile money**.
- The receipt shows an **OFF-…** number. Print it from the sale screen; the official number is given when the sale is sent.
- Not available offline: mobile money prompts (type the transaction reference instead), held orders, adding new customers, returns, reports.

## When it returns
Waiting sales are sent automatically, oldest first, and the bar shows how many are waiting. Open it to see them, send now, try again or remove one. A sale is never recorded twice.

## What to check afterwards
A sale that was made offline is **never refused for stock**. If the shelf count was lower than the till believed, stock goes below zero and the sale gets a **Check** mark. The same happens if a price changed while you were offline or a payment no longer fits. Open **Sales → All sales → Offline sales to check**, look at the reason, fix the stock or the money, and press **Mark as checked**.

## Good to know
- Sign in once before the connection is lost. If your sign-in expires while offline, sign in again later; waiting sales stay safe on the device.
- Use the same browser and computer that made the sales until they are sent. Clearing browser data removes unsent sales.
- Signing out removes the saved product list from the device, but not unsent sales.
- Sales older than 30 days are refused.
