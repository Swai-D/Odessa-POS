When your shop has AzamPay connected (**Settings → Integrations → Mobile money**), a mobile money line in the payment screen shows a network, a phone number and a **Request payment** button.

## Take a payment
1. Add the products and press **Pay**. Choose **Mobile money** (or add a mobile money line to a split payment).
2. Pick the network, type the customer's phone number, check the amount, and press **Request payment**.
3. The customer gets a prompt on their phone and enters their PIN. The screen says **Waiting…** and updates by itself.
4. When it says **Paid**, press the confirm button. The sale is saved with that payment.

## If it does not work
- **Failed** or **did not approve in time**: press **Try again**, or remove the line and take another payment.
- You cannot finish the sale while a request is waiting. Wait, or remove the line.
- A payment can pay only one sale, and only for the exact amount asked.
- No AzamPay, or the customer paid some other way? Leave the phone fields alone and type the reference as before. That line is recorded by hand.
