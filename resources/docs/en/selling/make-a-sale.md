The till (**POS**) is where you ring up customers. You need the POS permission, which Owner, Manager and Cashier have by default.

## Ring up a sale

1. In the side menu, under **Main**, click **POS**.
2. Add products. Click a product tile, or type a name, SKU or barcode in the search box. A barcode scanner types the code and presses Enter, which adds that exact product. Use the category tiles at the top to filter.
3. Change quantities with the **+** and **-** buttons or by typing a number in the **QTY** box. The trash icon removes a line.
4. Choose the customer, or leave **Walk-in customer**. Search by name or phone, or click the add-person icon to create one (see [Add a customer](help:customers/add-a-customer)). Selling on credit needs a customer.
5. Click **Pay**, check the amount, and click **Complete sale**. See [Payments and change](help:selling/payments-and-change).
6. The **Sale completed** box shows the receipt number. Click **View receipt**, **80mm receipt** or **New sale**.

## Discounts

Click the pencil icon next to **Discount**, choose **Percent (%)** or **Fixed amount**, type the value and click **Save**. The discount applies to the whole order and is shared across the lines. A percentage can be up to 100, and a discount can never be more than the sub total. To remove it, click the trash icon on the purple discount bar.

## What the till checks

- **Prices come from the product.** You cannot type a different price at the till; change it in **Products**.
- **Stock.** Each tile shows how much is in stock, and **Out of stock** is shown at zero. You cannot add more than the stock in the selected warehouse. The system checks again when you complete the sale.
- **Whole numbers.** Products sold by piece take whole quantities only. Weighed products and units that allow decimals (such as kg) accept values like 1.5.
- **Tax** is worked out from the product's tax settings and shown in the summary.
- A sale with a total of zero cannot be completed.

> **Tip:** If your shop has more than one warehouse, a warehouse list appears above the products. Choose the warehouse you are selling from before you add items. Changing it clears the cart.

> **Note:** **Clear all** and **Void** empty the cart after you confirm. To set a cart aside and serve someone else, use **Hold** instead. See [Hold and resume an order](help:selling/hold-and-resume).
