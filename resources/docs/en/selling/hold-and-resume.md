Use **Hold** when a customer is still choosing or has gone to fetch money, and you need the till for the next person.

## Hold a cart

1. Add the products (and customer or discount if you have them) as usual.
2. Click **Hold**. There is one beside **Pay** and another in the bar at the bottom.
3. Optionally type a label such as "Mama Asha, blue bag". It helps you find the order later.
4. Click **Hold**. The cart is saved and the till clears, ready for the next sale.

A held order keeps the products, quantities, customer and discount. It does not keep payments.

## Resume or discard

1. Click **Held Orders** in the bar at the bottom.
2. Find the order by its label, customer, number of items or time.
3. Click **Resume** to bring it back into the cart, or **Discard** to delete it.
4. Continue with **Pay** as normal.

> **Note:** A held order can be resumed only once. Resuming removes it from the list. If you hold it again, it is a new held order.

## Good to know

- **Stock is not reserved.** Held items can still be sold to someone else. When you resume, the till checks current stock and prices. An item with not enough stock is not added back and you see a warning.
- **The cart is replaced.** Resuming loads the held order in place of whatever is in the cart now, so finish or hold your current sale first.
- **Who can see what.** A cashier sees only the orders they held themselves. People who can manage sales (Owner, Manager and Accountant by default) see everyone's held orders.
- A held order does not appear in **Sales** or in your totals until it is paid. It does not expire, so discard orders you no longer need.
- The **Hold** button needs at least one item in the cart.

> **Tip:** Always give busy-day orders a label. "#12" is all you see otherwise.

If the till says a product is no longer available when you resume, it was deactivated or deleted. Remove it and continue, or see [Not enough stock](help:troubleshooting/not-enough-stock).
