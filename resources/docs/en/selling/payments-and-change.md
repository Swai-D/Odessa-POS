After you add products at the till, the **Pay** button opens **Select Payment**. This guide covers every way of paying except credit; for that, see [Credit sales](help:selling/credit-sales).

## Pay with one method

1. At the till, click one of the payment tiles (**Cash**, **Card**, **Mobile Money**, **Bank Transfer** or **Cheque**), or click **Pay**, which starts with cash.
2. The box shows the **Total Payable** at the top and a row with the method and the amount. The amount is already filled in with the full total.
3. For cash, type the money the customer gave you in the **Amount** box. The line **Change** shows what to give back.
4. For other methods you can type a **Reference (optional)**, such as the mobile money transaction code or the cheque number. It is printed in the sale details.
5. Click **Complete sale**.

> **Note:** Mobile Money, Card, Cheque and Bank Transfer are only records of how the customer paid. The system does not contact the mobile money company or the bank; you must check that the money arrived yourself.

## Split a bill

Use **Split Bill** when a customer pays part in cash and part by mobile money, for example.

1. Click **Split Bill**. Two rows appear: one for cash and one for mobile money.
2. Change the method in each row with the list, and type the amount for each.
3. Click **Add payment** for another row; the trash icon removes a row.
4. Watch the three lines at the bottom: **Paid**, **Change** and **Balance due on credit**. When **Paid** equals the total and the balance is zero, click **Complete sale**.

## Rules for change

- **Only cash can be more than the total.** The extra cash is change. For card, mobile money, cheque and bank transfer the amount cannot be more than what is due; the till shows an error if it is.
- If the customer pays less than the total, the rest is a balance on credit. That needs a customer. See [Credit sales](help:selling/credit-sales).
- Stock is deducted and the receipt number is created only when you click **Complete sale**. If your connection drops, the cart is kept and you can try again; the same sale is not charged twice.

> **Tip:** After the sale, the **Sale completed** box shows the change again, so you can read it out to the customer. See [Receipts and the sales list](help:selling/receipts-and-sales-list) to print the receipt.
