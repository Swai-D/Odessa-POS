Every completed sale is kept in **Sales** with its receipt. You need the permission to view sales; Owner, Manager, Cashier and Accountant have it.

## Find a sale

1. Click **Sales** in the side menu.
2. Type in the search box to find a receipt number or a customer name.
3. Use the filter to switch between **All sales** and **With balance due**.
4. Click the column titles **Receipt No.**, **Date**, **Total** or **Balance** to sort.
5. Click the receipt number (or the eye icon) to open the sale.

The list shows the **Customer**, the **Cashier**, **Total**, **Paid**, **Balance** and **Status** (**Paid**, **Partly paid** or **Unpaid**).

## Print or download the receipt

On the receipt page choose the format first:

- **A4 invoice** for a full page.
- **80mm receipt** for a thermal till printer.

Then click **Print** to print from the browser, or **Download PDF** to save a file you can send to the customer. If the printer integration is switched on, a thermal print button also appears; see [Integrations](help:settings/integrations).

The receipt shows the business name, the items, discount, tax, **Total**, what was **Paid**, the **Change**, and the footer text you set in **Settings**. See [Shop settings](help:settings/shop-settings). If items were returned, it also shows **Returned** and **Net after returns**, and any payments recorded later are listed under **Payments**.

## Other things on this page

- **Record payment** appears when there is a balance. See [Credit sales](help:selling/credit-sales).
- **Return items** appears when something can still be returned. See [Returns and refunds](help:selling/returns-and-refunds).

> **Tip:** The search box at the top of every page also finds sales by receipt number.

> **Note:** A sale cannot be edited or deleted after it is completed. To correct a mistake, record a return.
