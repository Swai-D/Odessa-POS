Suppliers are the businesses you buy stock from. You need a supplier before you can record a purchase. This is part of the Medium plan and above.

## Add a supplier

1. In the side menu, under **Peoples**, click **Suppliers**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Type the **Name** (required). You can also fill in **Phone**, **Email** and **Address**.
4. Keep **Active** switched on and click **Save**.

## Find, edit and see what you owe

- Use the search box to find a supplier by name.
- Click the pencil icon to change details, then **Save**.
- The list shows **We owe**: the unpaid balance of all purchases from that supplier. See [Pay a supplier](help:purchasing/pay-a-supplier).

## Deleting a supplier

A supplier who has purchases cannot be deleted: you get **This supplier has purchases and cannot be deleted.** Switch **Active** off instead; they stay in your history but no longer clutter new work.

## Who can do this

Owner and Manager can add and edit suppliers; the Storekeeper can too. The Accountant can only look. See [Users and roles](help:settings/users-and-roles).
