Purchases you did not pay in full stay open with a balance. Record each payment you make so you always know what you owe. Needs the Medium plan and the permission to manage purchases.

## See what you owe

- **Purchases**: filter by **Unpaid only**, or look at the **Balance** and **Status** columns.
- **Suppliers**: the **We owe** column adds up everything owed to each supplier.
- **Reports**: the Purchases section shows a payable total. See [Reports](help:money/reports).

## Record a payment

1. Open **Purchases** and click the purchase number.
2. Under **Record payment** (the box shows the current **Balance**), choose the **Method**, type the **Amount** and a **Payment reference** if you have one (for example a transaction code).
3. Click **Record payment**.

The payment appears under **Payments to supplier**, and **Paid** and **Balance** update. When the balance is zero the status becomes **Paid**.

## Rules

- A payment cannot be more than the balance: **The payment is more than the balance due.**
- A fully paid purchase says **This purchase is already fully paid.**
- You can pay in parts as many times as you need.

> **Note:** Paying a supplier from the cash drawer is not tracked by the till closing. If you pay a supplier in cash from the till, the closing will show a difference of that amount. See [Close the till](help:money/close-the-till).

> **Tip:** Record the payment on the day you pay, using the same reference as your mobile money or bank message, so you can match them later.
