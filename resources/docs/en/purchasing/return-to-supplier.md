When goods you bought are damaged, wrong or not wanted, you can send them back and record it. Open the purchase (**Purchases**, then the purchase number) and use **Return goods to supplier**.

## How to do it

1. Next to each product, type the **quantity** you are sending back. Leave 0 for products you are keeping.
2. If the supplier will give money back (not just reduce what you owe), choose **how you received it**.
3. Add a **reason** if you wish, then click **Send goods back**.

## What happens

- The stock **leaves the warehouse** the goods were received into, and the movement is in stock history as "Purchase return".
- The value of the goods first **reduces what you still owe** on that purchase. If the purchase was already fully paid, the whole value is money the supplier gives back, so choose the method.
- The purchase shows the returned amount and the net total, and each return has its own number (PR-000001...).

## Things to know

- You cannot return more than you received, and you cannot return goods that have already been sold. The stock must still be in the warehouse; otherwise nothing is changed.
- You can return a purchase in several steps.
- Returns are in the audit log.
