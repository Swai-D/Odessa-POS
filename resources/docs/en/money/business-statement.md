When you apply for a loan, a bank or lender wants to see how your business has been doing. The **business statement** gives them that in one document, made from the sales, expenses and stock you already record in Odessa POS. Open **Reports > Business statement**.

## Create one

1. Choose the **period**: the last 3, 6 or 12 whole months before today. A month that is still running is not included.
2. Choose the **language** of the document (English or Kiswahili).
3. Click **Create statement**. Then **download the PDF** or **print** it.

## What it shows

- A summary: net sales, average net sales per month, gross profit, expenses, net profit, number of sales and customers, and the months you traded.
- A **month-by-month table** of sales, returns, profit, expenses and purchases.
- Your position on the day it was issued: what customers owe you, what you owe suppliers, and stock at cost.
- Your main expenses.

The figures are the same ones the Reports page gives for the same dates.

## How the lender can check it

Every statement has a **number** and a **verification code**. The lender opens the **Verify** page (the address is printed on the paper), types the code and sees the headline figures to compare with the paper. If the paper was changed, the figures will not match.

## Good to know

- A statement is **fixed** when it is created. To show newer figures, create a new one; the old ones stay in the list.
- It only contains what was **recorded** in the system. Sales that were not entered are not included, so keep recording every sale.
- It is **not audited** and does not replace audited accounts. Each lender decides what it accepts, so ask what they want to see.
- Creating a statement is recorded in the audit log.
