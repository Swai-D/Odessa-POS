Expenses are the money you spend to run the shop, apart from buying stock. Recording them lets [Reports](help:money/reports) show your real net profit. This is part of the Medium plan and above. Owner, Manager and Accountant can record expenses.

## Set up categories first

1. In the side menu, under **Finance**, click **Expense categories**.
2. Click **Add**, type a **Name** (for example Rent, Electricity, Wages, Transport) and **Save**.
3. A category that already has expenses cannot be deleted: **This category has expenses and cannot be deleted. Switch it off instead.**

You can also reach this page from the **Manage categories** link inside the expense form.

## Add an expense

1. Click **Finance > Expenses**, then **Add expense**.
2. Choose the **Category**.
3. Type the **Amount** in your currency.
4. Set the **Date** (today or earlier; you cannot enter a future date).
5. Choose **Paid by** (cash, mobile money and so on), and add a **Reference** and **Note** if useful.
6. Click **Save**.

## Find, change and export

- The filters **From**, **To** and **Category**, then **Apply**, show one period. The default is this month. The **Total** above the table adds up what you see.
- The search box looks in the reference, note and category.
- Click the column titles **Date** or **Amount** to sort.
- Use the pencil to change an expense and the trash icon to delete it.
- The download icon at the top saves the list as a CSV file that opens in Excel.

> **Note:** Expenses paid in cash from the till drawer are not taken off the cash count when you close the till. See [Close the till](help:money/close-the-till).

> **Tip:** Record expenses the day they happen. A monthly look at **Expenses** next to your sales tells you more than any guess.
