If you sell from more than one place, the branch report shows how each one is doing next to the others. In Odessa POS a **branch** is a warehouse: a sale belongs to the warehouse it was made from.

## Open the report

1. In the sidebar, under **Reports**, open **Branch report**. It needs the Enterprise plan and the reports permission.
2. Choose **From** and **To** dates and press **Show**. The default is the last 30 days.
3. To keep the figures, press **Download CSV**. It opens in Excel.

## What each column means

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sales** | Number of sales in the period. |
| **Sales amount** | Total of those sales. |
| **Returns** | Money for items returned in the period, taken off the branch where the sale was made. |
| **Net sales** | Sales amount minus returns. Branches are sorted by this, best first. |
| **Average sale** | Sales amount divided by the number of sales. |
| **Gross profit** | Selling price minus the cost price recorded at the time of sale, after returns and before expenses. |
| **Purchases** | Value of purchases received into that branch in the period. |
| **Stock value (cost)** | What the stock in that branch is worth at cost price right now, with the number of units in brackets. |
| **Share of net sales** | The branch's part of all your net sales. |

The last row adds up all branches.

## Good to know

- Expenses are not tied to a branch, so profit here is shown before expenses. For your shop's full profit and loss, see [Read your reports](help:money/reports).
- Stock value is as of now, not at the end of the period you picked.
- A branch that is switched off and has no sales or stock is left out.
- To add a branch, create a warehouse. Your plan decides how many you may have. See [Plans compared](help:plans/plans-compared).
