**Reports** gives the owner a clear picture of the shop. It is part of the Medium plan and above. Owner, Manager and Accountant can open it.

## Choose the period

1. Click **Reports** in the side menu.
2. Set **From** and **To**, then click **Apply**. The default is the last 30 days; the longest period is one year.

## What you will see

- **Four cards**: **Net sales** (after returns), **Returns**, **Sold on credit** and **Collected at sale**. The small note explains gross sales, discounts and tax.
- **Best sellers**: quantity, revenue and profit per product. Revenue and profit leave out tax and are net of returns.
- **Payments by method**: how much came in as cash, mobile money and so on.
- **Customers who owe** and **Stock** (value at cost, units on hand, and **Running low** products). These show the situation now, not for the period.
- **Profit and loss**: revenue, **Cost of goods sold**, **Gross profit**, **Expenses** by category and **Net profit**. It appears when you can see expenses; see [Expenses](help:money/expenses). Purchases count as cost only when the stock is sold.
- **Purchases**: what you bought in the period and what you still **owe suppliers**.
- **Sales by day**: a table for each day.

## Download as CSV

The **CSV** buttons save **Best sellers**, **Payments by method**, **Customers who owe**, **Running low** stock and **Sales by day** as files you can open in Excel.

## Tips for reading the numbers

- Profit depends on the cost price of your products. Keep it right by ticking **Update product cost prices to these costs** when you [record a purchase](help:purchasing/record-a-purchase), or edit the product.
- A product whose cost price is 0 shows its full revenue as profit.
- Reports use the dates when sales were made, not when they were paid.

> **Tip:** Check the same report on the first day of every month, and compare it with the previous month.
