Categories, brands and units help you group and filter products. They are all managed the same way, from a list page with a pop-up form.

## Categories

1. Open **Inventory > Categories** and click **Add**.
2. Type the **Name**. The **Slug** is optional; leave it empty and one is made from the name.
3. To make a sub-category, choose a **Parent Category**.
4. Keep **Active** on and click **Save**.

Use the pencil icon to edit and the bin icon to delete. A category cannot be deleted while products use it or while it has sub-categories. Switch **Active** off instead.

## Brands

Brands need the Medium plan; on Basic the **Brands** menu is hidden and the Brand field is missing from the product form. See [Plans compared](help:plans/plans-compared).

1. Open **Inventory > Brands** and click **Add**.
2. Type the **Name** (it must be unique) and click **Save**.

## Units

A unit tells the system how an item is counted. Your shop starts with **Piece**, **Kilogram** and **Litre**.

1. Open **Inventory > Units** and click **Add**.
2. Enter the **Name** (for example Box) and the **Short Name** (box). Both are required.
3. Switch **Allow Decimals** on if the item can be sold in parts, such as 1.5 kg. Leave it off for items counted in whole pieces.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** If a product's unit does not allow decimals, you can only sell, receive or transfer it in whole numbers.

## Using them

Choose the category, brand and unit when you [add a product](help:products/add-a-product). On **Inventory > Products** you can filter the list by category or brand. Import files also create missing categories, brands and units automatically; see [Import and export products](help:products/import-export).

> **Tip:** You need the matching permission to change these lists. Owners and Managers have it. A Storekeeper can view them but not change them.
