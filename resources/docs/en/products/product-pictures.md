A picture makes a product easy to recognise in lists. Open **Products**, then add or edit a product, and choose a file under **Picture**.

## What to know

- Use a **JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF** file of up to 5 MB. You do not need to resize it first: the system shrinks it (longest side 800 pixels) and saves it in a small format.
- One picture per product. Uploading a new one **replaces** the old one; tick **Remove the picture** to take it off.
- The picture shows next to the product name in the product list.

## Storage

Pictures are kept in your shop's own storage, and each plan has an allowance (shown on **Billing** under your limits). Shrunk pictures are small, so hundreds fit in the smallest plan. When your storage is full you are told, and you can remove pictures you no longer need or ask for a bigger plan. Nothing already saved is ever deleted because of the limit.
