Labels let you scan products at the till instead of typing or searching.

## Print labels
1. Open **Products** and click the barcode icon at the top (or on a product's row to start with that product).
2. Search and add products. Set **Copies** for each, or press **Copies = stock on hand** after receiving goods.
3. Choose the **Label size**: a label roll (50×30, 40×25, 58×40 mm) or an A4 sticker sheet (24, 40 or 65 per sheet).
4. Tick what must appear: shop name, product name, price, SKU.
5. Press **Print labels**. In the print window pick your label printer, set margins to **None** and scale to **100%**.

## What barcode is printed
- The product's **Barcode** if it has one (EAN-13, UPC-A and EAN-8 are drawn in the normal retail style).
- Otherwise its **SKU**, printed as a Code 128 barcode. The till finds products by SKU too, so these scan correctly.

## Tips
- A partly used A4 sheet: enter how many positions to **skip** so you do not print onto stickers already used.
- Print one test label and scan it with your scanner before printing a whole roll.
- A product whose SKU has special characters (for example accents) cannot be drawn as a barcode; give it a plain Barcode or SKU.
