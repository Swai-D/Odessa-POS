A warehouse is any place that holds your stock: a shop floor, a storeroom or a second branch. Stock is always counted per warehouse. Your shop starts with one called **Main Store**.

You need the warehouses permission to change them (Owner and Manager by default). Storekeepers can only look.

## Add a warehouse

1. Open **Inventory > Warehouses** and click **Add**.
2. Enter the **Name** and a **Code** (required, up to 30 characters, unique, for example MAIN or BR2).
3. Optionally fill **Address** and **Phone**.
4. Switch **Default** on if this should be your main warehouse. Only one warehouse can be the default, so choosing a new one switches the old one off.
5. Keep **Active** on and click **Save**.

## Plan limits

| Plan | Warehouses |
|---|---|
| Basic | 1 |
| Medium | 3 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

If you reach the limit you see "Your plan allows up to N warehouses. Contact us to upgrade." See [Plans compared](help:plans/plans-compared). Moving stock between warehouses needs at least two, so [Transfer stock](help:stock/transfer-stock) is not available on Basic.

## What the default does

The default warehouse is chosen first at the till and is the one used when you import opening stock. When you create a product, [Add a product](help:products/add-a-product) lets you pick the warehouse for opening stock.

## Edit, switch off or delete

- Use the pencil icon to change details.
- Switch **Active** off to hide a warehouse from sales, purchases, adjustments and transfers. Its stock history stays.
- A warehouse that has ever had stock movements cannot be deleted. Switch it off instead.

> **Note:** Switching a warehouse off does not move its stock, and an inactive warehouse still counts toward your plan's limit. Transfer the stock out first if you are closing it.

> **Tip:** Keep codes short and meaningful so they are easy to recognise in lists. See [Stock levels](help:stock/stock-levels) to check what each warehouse holds.
