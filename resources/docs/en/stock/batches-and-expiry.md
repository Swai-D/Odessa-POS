Use this for goods that go off (food, medicine, cosmetics) or that you want to trace to a supplier's lot.

## Switch it on
1. **Settings → Optional features**: turn on **Batch tracking** and/or **Expiry tracking**.
2. Open the product and switch on **Track batches** / **Track expiry**.

## Enter the details when stock arrives
- **Purchases**: each line has **Batch no.** and **Expiry date** (only for products that track them). Two lines of the same product with different dates become separate batches.
- **New product with opening stock**: fill the opening batch number and expiry date.
- **Stock adjustment (add)**: the same two fields.
- **Transfers** between warehouses carry the batch number and expiry date along.

## What the system does with them
- **Sells the batch that expires first**, even if it arrived later. Batches without a date are sold last. Products that do not track expiry keep the oldest-delivery-first rule.
- **Stock → Batches** shows the batch number and expiry date of every batch. Use **Expiring in 30 days** and **Expired** to see what needs attention.
- **At the till**, a product shows an amber or red label with the date its oldest stock goes off, and a warning appears if you add a product whose oldest batch has already expired. The sale is not blocked: you decide.

## Tips
- Dates are optional. Stock without a date is never flagged.
- Existing stock has no dates. Add them with a stock adjustment, or leave them for the next delivery.
