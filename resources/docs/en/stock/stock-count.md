A stock count checks the quantities in the system against what is really on your shelves, and corrects the differences. Open **Stock > Stock Count**. You need the Inventory permission to start one.

## How to count

1. Click **New stock count**. Choose the **warehouse**. To count only one shelf or type of goods, choose a **category**; otherwise every stock-tracked product is included.
2. A sheet opens with every product and **what the system says** you have.
3. Walk the shelves and type the quantity you counted for each product. **Leave a box empty for any product you did not count**: it will not change.
4. Click **Save counts** as you go. A big shop has several pages of 100 products: save each page before moving to the next.
5. When you are done, click **Complete and correct stock**. Every counted product that differs gets an adjustment, so the system matches your shelves. You can see them in **Stock Adjustments** and in the audit log.

## Good to know

- **Count at the moment you start.** The difference is your count minus what the system expected when you opened the sheet, so sales made while you count stay correct. If you count hours later and sales happened in between, count again or adjust by hand.
- **One count at a time per warehouse.** Finish or cancel the open one first.
- **Completing cannot be undone.** If you made a mistake, fix it with a normal adjustment, see [Adjust stock](help:stock/adjust-stock).
- **Cancel** a count to throw the sheet away. Nothing in your stock changes.
- A product sold in whole units needs a whole number. Products sold by weight accept decimals.
- After completing, use **Only differences** to see what was wrong.
