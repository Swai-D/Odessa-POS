The subscription is paid monthly. Each shop has a **paid until** date, and that date drives the reminders and what you can do.

## The stages

| Stage | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Up to the paid-until date | Everything works. From 7 days before the date, a reminder appears (top bell and a banner). |
| **Grace period** | The 7 days after the date | Everything still works. A banner asks you to renew by a given date. |
| **Read-only** | After the grace period | You can look at everything and print, but you cannot sell or change anything until you renew. |
| **Suspended** | The platform administrator switches the shop off | The shop is closed until it is switched back on. |

A shop with no paid-until date has no deadline.

## What read-only means

- You can still sign in, open sales, reports, stock and customers, and print or download receipts.
- Anything that saves or changes data is refused: new sales, payments, products, stock, users and settings.
- Your data is safe. **Nothing is deleted.**

See [Why is my shop read-only?](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).

## Renew

1. Pay the person or company that supplies the system. They tell you the amount.
2. They record the payment for one or more months, and the paid-until date moves forward.
3. The banners go away and you can sell again. If you were read-only, this happens at once; you do not need to sign out.

If you pay before the date, the new period starts where your current one ends, so you do not lose days. If you pay late, it starts on the day you paid, so the days in between are not free.

> **Tip:** Renew during the reminder week, not the grace week, so the till never shows warnings in front of customers.

> **Note:** A shop that was switched off (Suspended) stays off after a payment until the platform administrator switches it back on.

> **Note:** Automatic charging from mobile money is not available yet. Renewal is recorded by the platform administrator.
