Odessa POS has three plans. All of them include the till, products, stock, sales, customers, settings, receipts and till closing. The plan decides which extra features are switched on and how many users and warehouses you may have.

## Comparison

| | Basic | Medium | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Till (POS), products, stock, sales, customers | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Sale returns and refunds | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Credit sales | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Till closing | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Brands | No | Yes | Yes |
| Purchasing and suppliers | No | Yes | Yes |
| Expenses and profit and loss | No | Yes | Yes |
| Reports | No | Yes | Yes |
| Integrations (receipt printer) | No | Yes | Yes |
| Users | up to 3 | up to 10 | unlimited |
| Warehouses | 1 | up to 3 | unlimited |

Enterprise also reserves **TRA fiscal receipts**, but that connection is not available yet. See [Integrations](help:settings/integrations).

## How you see what is locked

A menu item that your plan does not include stays in the side menu, dimmed with a small lock. Click it and a page tells you which plan has it and who to contact. Nothing is hidden by mistake and nothing breaks.

## Limits

- **Users:** the **Users** page shows, for example, "3 of 3 users". At the limit, adding another user is refused with a message that your plan allows up to that many.
- **Warehouses:** the same for **Warehouses**. Stock transfers need at least two warehouses, so they need the Medium plan or higher. See [Transfer stock](help:stock/transfer-stock).

## Change your plan

Contact whoever supplies the system to you. The phone number or email is shown on the upgrade page. They change the plan and the new features appear straight away; your data is untouched.

> **Note:** Data is never deleted when a plan changes. If a shop moves down to a smaller plan, the extra features are only switched off.
