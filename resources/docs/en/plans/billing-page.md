Open **Settings > Subscription & billing** (Owner only) to see everything about your subscription in one place.

## What you will see

- **Your plan and its status.** Active, Grace period or Read-only, with the date you have **paid until** and a live **countdown** in days, hours, minutes and seconds. A bar shows how much of the paid period has gone.
- **How to pay.** The mobile-money numbers and bank account to pay to, with simple steps. Use your **shop name** as the reference so we can find your payment.
- **All plans and their features**, with prices per month and per year. Your own plan is marked.
- **Payment history.** Every payment we have recorded for your shop: date, plan, period covered, amount, method and reference.

## How to renew

1. Pay the amount for your plan using one of the methods on the page.
2. Keep the confirmation SMS and send us the transaction ID, or call us.
3. Our team confirms the payment and renews your subscription, usually the same working day. The new date then shows on the page and in your payment history.

> **Note:** The page does not take payment by itself. Renewal is recorded by our team after we confirm your payment. See [Renewal, grace period and read-only mode](help:plans/renewal-and-grace) for what happens if you pay late, and [What each plan includes](help:plans/plans-compared) to compare plans.

To change plan, tell us when you pay and we will switch it for you.
