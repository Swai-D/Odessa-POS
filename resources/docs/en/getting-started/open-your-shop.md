You do not need to wait for anyone to open a shop for you.

## Start a trial

1. On the sign-in page click **New here? Start a free trial**.
2. Enter the **shop name**, **your name**, your **email** and a **password** of at least 8 characters.
3. Tick that you agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy, then click **Start free trial**.

You are signed in at once, as the **Owner** of your new shop. A **Main Store** warehouse and the usual units (piece, kilogram, litre) are already there, and the setup checklist on the dashboard shows what to do next.

## During the trial

- The trial lasts a number of days (shown on the sign-up page) and gives you **every feature**, so you see the reports and profit you would get by subscribing.
- No card or payment is asked for.
- When the trial ends you keep a short grace period, then the shop becomes **read-only**. Your data is never deleted; open **Billing** to see how to pay and continue.

## Good to know

- One email address belongs to one shop. If you already have an account, sign in instead.
- Your shop address is made from the shop name; if the name is taken, a few letters are added.
- To add staff, use **Settings > Users**.
