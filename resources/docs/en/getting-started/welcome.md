Odessa POS is a till and shop-management system. You sell at the counter, and the same system keeps your stock, customer credit and daily totals up to date.

## What you can do

- **Sell fast.** Search or scan products, take payment, give change and print a receipt. See [Make a sale](help:selling/make-a-sale).
- **Keep stock correct.** Every sale reduces stock, and every return puts it back. See [Stock levels](help:stock/stock-levels).
- **Sell on credit.** Let trusted customers pay later and track what they owe. See [Credit sales](help:selling/credit-sales).
- **Know your numbers.** The **Dashboard** shows today's sales, returns, credit given and how much customers owe.
- **Grow with a bigger plan.** Purchasing, suppliers, expenses, reports and mobile money come with the Medium plan and above. See [Plans compared](help:plans/plans-compared).

## How your shop is organised

Each shop has its own private data, products and staff. Everyone signs in with their own email and password, and what they can see depends on their role:

| Role | Usually does |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including **Settings** and **Users** |
| Manager | Everything except **Settings** |
| Cashier | Sells at the till, views sales, manages customers |
| Storekeeper | Products, stock, purchases and suppliers |
| Accountant | Sales, payments, reports and expenses |

If a menu is missing for you, read [I cannot see a menu](help:troubleshooting/cannot-see-a-menu).

## Things to know

- Money is in **TZS** by default. The Owner can change the currency in **Settings**.
- **Mobile Money**, **Card**, **Cheque** and **Bank Transfer** are recorded as payment methods. You type the amount and an optional reference yourself; Odessa POS does not collect the money from the customer's phone or bank.
- Receipts are normal shop receipts. Fiscal (TRA) receipts are not part of the app.
- You can switch between English and Kiswahili at any time with the language icon at the top of the screen.

> **Tip:** Click the question-mark icon at the top of any page to open this Help centre, then type what you want to do, for example "refund".
