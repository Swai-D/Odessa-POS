Follow this checklist the first time you use Odessa POS. It takes about 15 minutes. Steps 2 and 5 need the Owner account.

## Checklist

1. **Sign in** with your email address and password. Choose your language with the language icon at the top (English or Kiswahili).
2. **Fill in your shop details.** Open **Settings** and enter the **Business name**, choose the **Currency** (TZS, KES, UGX, USD or EUR) and, if you like, a **Receipt footer** such as "Thank you, come again". Click **Save**. The business name and footer appear on every receipt.
3. **Check your warehouse.** Your shop starts with one warehouse called "Main Store". Look at it under **Inventory > Warehouses**. The Basic plan allows one warehouse; see [Warehouses](help:stock/warehouses).
4. **Add your products.** Go to **Inventory > Products** and click **Add Product**. Enter the name, selling price and **Opening Stock** so the till knows what you have. For many items, use [Import and export](help:products/import-export). Start with [Add a product](help:products/add-a-product).
5. **Add your staff.** Open **Settings > Users**, click **Add**, and give each person a role (Cashier, Manager and so on). The Basic plan allows 3 users. See [Users and roles](help:settings/users-and-roles).
6. **Add customers** you will sell to on credit. See [Add a customer](help:customers/add-a-customer).
7. **Make a test sale.** Open **POS**, tap a product, press **Pay**, then **Complete sale**. Open the receipt and check the business name and footer. See [Make a sale](help:selling/make-a-sale).

> **Note:** A test sale is a real sale. It reduces stock and shows in your totals. Use a returned item to undo it: [Returns and refunds](help:selling/returns-and-refunds).

## Settings you can leave for later

- The **Optional features** switches in **Settings** (printer, barcode scanner, cash drawer and so on) are off until you turn them on. Most shops do not need them on day one.
- Categories, brands and units help you organise products. See [Categories, brands and units](help:products/categories-brands-units).

> **Tip:** A cashier signing in sees a shorter menu than the Owner. That is normal; menus follow the role. See [Find your way around](help:getting-started/find-your-way).
