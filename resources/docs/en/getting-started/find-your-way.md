Every page has a side menu on the left and a bar along the top. What you see depends on your role and your plan.

## The side menu

| Section | Items |
|---|---|
| Main | **Dashboard**, **POS** |
| Inventory | **Products**, **Categories**, **Brands**, **Units**, **Warehouses** |
| Stock | **Stock Levels**, **Stock Transfers**, **Stock Adjustments** |
| Sales | **Sales** |
| Purchases | **Purchases** |
| Peoples | **Customers**, **Suppliers** |
| Finance | **Expenses**, **Expense categories** |
| Reports | **Reports** |
| Settings | **Settings**, **Users**, **Integrations** |

**Brands**, **Purchases**, **Suppliers**, **Expenses**, **Expense categories**, **Reports** and **Integrations** need the Medium plan. If you are on Basic they are hidden. See [Plans compared](help:plans/plans-compared).

A cashier normally sees only **Dashboard**, **POS**, **Products**, **Sales** and **Customers**. If a menu you need is missing, see [I cannot see a menu](help:troubleshooting/cannot-see-a-menu).

## The top bar

- **Search box.** Type at least 2 letters to search products, customers, sales and purchases at once.
- **Add.** A quick list of "new" shortcuts (new product, customer and so on) that you are allowed to use.
- **POS.** Opens the till straight away.
- **Language icon.** Switch between English and Kiswahili. Your choice is remembered for your account.
- **Question-mark icon.** Opens this Help centre.
- **Bell.** Shows products that are running low and any subscription notice. See [Renewal and grace period](help:plans/renewal-and-grace).
- **Your profile menu.** **My Profile** (change your name or password), **Settings** and **Logout**.

## The Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your landing page. It shows **Sales today**, **Returns today**, **Credit given today**, **Customers owe**, a 7-day sales table and your **Recent sales**. With Medium it also shows **Expenses this month**. A low-stock warning has a **View stock** link.

> **Tip:** A coloured bar at the top of the page means your subscription is close to ending or has ended. Read it, because once it ends the shop becomes read-only. See [Read-only mode](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).

## Lists and forms

- Most lists have a search box and clickable column headings for sorting.
- Short forms such as categories and customers open in a pop-up. Click **Add** to create and the pencil icon to edit.
- Required fields have a red star.
